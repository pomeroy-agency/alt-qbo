Payroll

Prepared forms to review

941, 940, W-2 and state quarterly figures (such as California's DE 9 and DE 9C) prepared from the pay runs, with deadline reminders. You or the client file.

A pay run needs a second person to approve it before it posts to the books. That second person can be you.

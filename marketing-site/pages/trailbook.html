It prepares. You stay in charge of the money.

TrailBook never holds your money and never files on your behalf. It works out your payroll and sales tax, prepares the forms and reminds you before they are due.

Questions owners ask first

What happens after the 30-day trial?

Add a card and keep going on the plan you picked. If you don't, nothing is deleted: your books stay readable and downloadable, and only changes pause until you subscribe.

Does TrailBook pay my employees or file my taxes?

No. TrailBook calculates payroll, prepares the forms and builds a direct-deposit file for your bank. You pay your people and file the returns, or your accountant does. That keeps your money in your own account the whole time.

Do I need an accountant to use it?

No. Many owners run TrailBook on their own and bring their accountant in at tax time with a free login. If you would like a hand getting started, a guided setup call is $99, once.

Is my data safe?

Every change is recorded in an audit trail, sign-in supports a second step, and your books are backed up every night. You can download everything at any time. See how we keep your books safe.

Who makes TrailBook?

Pomeroy Agency, the trade name of Harmony in the Wild, LLC. We build systems for owner-run businesses, and TrailBook is our bookkeeping software. If you would rather have the whole system built around it, see Systems & Automation.

Which states does it work in?

TrailBook is for businesses based in the United States, with payroll rules built in for all 50 states and the District of Columbia. Canada, with all 10 provinces and 3 territories, is coming soon. See what is included.

Pomeroy Agency · Privacy

Privacy Policy

What we collect on pomeroyagency.com, why, who we share it with, and how to opt out or ask for your data. Effective October 28, 2026.

This policy covers pomeroyagency.com and the services we sell through it. Pomeroy Agency is the trade name of Harmony in the Wild, LLC, a California limited liability company ("we", "us"). TrailBook, our bookkeeping software at app.pomeroyagency.com, has its own privacy policy, and that policy governs your books and any data you put into TrailBook.

We do not collect Social Security numbers, bank account numbers or other sensitive personal information through this site, and the site is not directed to children under 16.

Analytics and advertising tools

We use these tools to understand which pages help people and to measure our advertising. They set cookies or similar identifiers in your browser.

  • Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC): measures visits and clicks. How Google uses this data. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Meta Pixel and Conversions API (Meta Platforms, Inc.): tells Meta when someone who saw one of our ads visits the site, clicks a button such as a free-trial or contact button, or asks for the scan results. When you submit your email for the scan, a one-way scrambled (SHA-256 hashed) copy of it is sent to Meta so the visit can be matched to an ad. We never send Meta your name, your scan answers or the plain email address. You can change how Meta uses this in your Meta ad preferences.
  • Wix (Wix.com Ltd.), which hosts the site, and the Visitor Analytics app inside it, record basic visit statistics.

We use this information for our own analytics and advertising. We do not sell your personal information for money. Sending identifiers to Meta and Google to measure and target ads may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law, and you can opt out of it as described below.

Who else receives your information

  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS), which runs our email forms and sends our emails.
  • Stripe (Stripe, Inc.), which processes payments.
  • Netlify (Netlify, Inc.), which hosts the Profit Leak Scan and our server-side analytics relay.
  • Google, Meta and Wix, as described above.
  • Professional advisers, and authorities when the law requires it.

Each of these companies processes data on its own servers, which may be outside your state or country.

Your choices

Opting out of analytics and ad tracking

  • Turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser. We treat a GPC signal as a request to opt out of sharing for advertising, for that browser.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use the Google and Meta links above.
  • Email gregory@pomeroyagency.com with the subject "Privacy opt-out".

Do Not Track. Browsers' older "Do Not Track" setting has no agreed standard, so the site does not change its behavior for it. Global Privacy Control is honored as described above.

Emails

Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing does not stop emails about work you have hired us for.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. California residents have these rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, and we extend them to everyone. We will not treat you differently for asking. Email gregory@pomeroyagency.com with the subject "Privacy request". We will confirm your request within 10 days and answer within 45 days, and we may need to verify that the request comes from you. You can use an authorized agent if you give them written permission.

How long we keep it

Email contacts stay on our list until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Client and payment records are kept for seven years for tax and accounting purposes. Analytics data is kept for 14 months in Google Analytics and as set by Meta and Wix.

Security

We use reputable providers, encrypted connections and two-step sign-in on our accounts. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we will tell you if a breach affects your information as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top. If a change is material, we will say so on this page before it takes effect.

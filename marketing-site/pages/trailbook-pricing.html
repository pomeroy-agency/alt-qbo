05

Questions

Do I pay sales tax on TrailBook?

If your business is in California: from January 1, 2027, California taxes software subscriptions, so your invoice adds sales tax at your address's rate. Before then there is none. In other states, we add sales tax only where we are required to collect it. Extras like setup calls are services and are billed separately.

What counts as a person paid?

Each employee who gets a paycheck from a pay run in that month. Nobody on payroll that month, no per-person charge. It is billed monthly in arrears on either plan interval.

I'm an accountant or HR firm. What do I pay?

Nothing for your firm's own logins. One sign-in switches between all your clients' books. Clients you bring get 20% off for as long as they stay, or your firm can take a 20% referral fee for their first 12 months instead. The client is always the customer of record. More on the firm program.

Who can use TrailBook?

Businesses based in one of the 13 US states TrailBook supports so far: California, Texas, Florida, Nevada, Tennessee, South Dakota, Wyoming, New Hampshire, Alaska, Washington, Arizona, Oregon and New York. Each comes with its state payroll rules built in, plus city minimum wages where they apply. More states are being added, and Canada is planned. Sales tax rates are looked up automatically in California; in the other states you set your rate yourself.

Questions from people switching

No. Keep QuickBooks running while you try TrailBook. Once your books are across and your first month reconciles, cancel QuickBooks on your own schedule.

The $249 move covers QuickBooks and Xero. Coming from anything else, you can start fresh at no cost, or book the $99 guided setup call and we set up your accounts and opening balances together.

30 days free. Cancel any time before day 30 and you pay nothing. On day 31 the plan you picked starts and renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel from the Billing page, no phone call.

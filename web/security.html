How we keep your books safe

What we do today, in plain words. We only list what is built and running.

Signing in

  • Two-factor sign-in with an authenticator app, plus one-time recovery codes. An owner can require it for everyone in the business.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted scrypt hashes. Emailed sign-in and reset links work once, expire quickly, and are stored only as hashes.
  • Sessions live in secure, HTTP-only cookies. Changing your password signs you out everywhere else.

Private details

  • Social Security numbers, bank account numbers and two-factor secrets are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are saved, with a key kept outside the database.
  • Everything travels over HTTPS.
  • Card numbers never reach us: you type them on Stripe's secure page, and we keep only the brand and last four digits.

Your business, kept apart

  • Every row of data belongs to one organisation, and the database itself (row-level security) hides every other organisation's rows.
  • Roles decide who can post, close months and approve payroll. Payroll needs two people: one prepares, another approves.
  • Every change goes in a hash-chained audit log you can check yourself.

Backups

  • Every night, a full copy of each business's books is encrypted and stored apart from the database.
  • Each copy is read back and checked the same night: it opens, nothing in it changed, every entry balances, and every account matches your live books. We keep 30 days of copies.
  • Owners, admins and accountants can download today's books, or any of the nightly copies, at any time.

Found a problem?

If you think you've found a security issue, please tell us before telling anyone else, and we'll reply within one business day. Contact: security@pomeroyagency.com.

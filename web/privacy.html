Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-29, in effect from October 29, 2026.

This policy explains how Harmony in the Wild, LLC (doing business as Pomeroy Agency), which operates TrailBook ("we" or "us"), handles personal information in TrailBook (the "Service") and on this website. TrailBook is accounting and payroll software for businesses. Our Terms of Service also apply.

Two kinds of information

Account information is about the people who use the Service: name, email, password (stored only as a hash), two-factor settings, sign-in records, and the roles you hold. For the business that pays, it also includes billing contact and billing address. We decide how this information is used.

Customer Data is what a business puts into its books. It can include personal information about that business's employees, contractors and customers, such as names, addresses, Social Security numbers, pay and bank account details. We handle Customer Data only on the business's instructions, to provide the Service. The business decides what goes in and is responsible for its own notices to those people.

How we use it

  • To provide the Service: keeping books, calculating payroll and tax, preparing forms, and sending the reminders and receipts you'd expect.
  • To keep it secure: sign-in checks, two-factor, the audit log, encrypted nightly backups, and investigating misuse.
  • To bill for it, and to keep the records the law requires, such as invoices and tax records.
  • To answer you when you contact us.
  • To measure our own advertising, as described under "Ad measurement" below.
  • To send TrailBook News, our weekly email, only if you sign up for it and confirm through the link we email you. Every issue has an unsubscribe link, and Brevo keeps the list.

We do not sell personal information. We don't show ads in the Service, and we don't use Customer Data to train outside AI models. Customer Data is never used for advertising.

Ad measurement

We advertise TrailBook on Google and Meta (Facebook and Instagram). To learn which ads bring in customers, when you create a new TrailBook account we tell Google and Meta that a sign-up happened, and when that business first pays, we tell them a payment happened and its amount. With each of those two events we send: the identifiers that Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel on pomeroyagency.com already stored in your browser, the ad click ID if you arrived from an ad, your IP address and browser type, and a scrambled (hashed) version of your TrailBook user ID. We never send your name, email address, business name or anything in your books.

Under California law this can count as "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can opt out: tick "Don't share my sign-up with Google or Meta" when you sign up, turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser (we treat it as an opt-out automatically), or email privacy@pomeroyagency.com with the subject "Do Not Share". We keep the IP address, browser type and browser identifiers only until the first payment is reported, or 120 days if there isn't one. Nothing is shared about people who join someone else's business by invitation, or about accounts created before October 29, 2026.

Security

Social Security numbers, bank account numbers and two-factor secrets are encrypted at rest. Every viewing of a full number is logged. More is on our security page. If a breach affects your information, we notify you as California law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep account information while the account is open. After a subscription ends, its books stay readable for 7 years, free, so the records are there for a tax or payroll audit (Terms, section 10). A business can ask us to delete its data sooner; we then delete it within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep. Backups age out after 30 days.

Your choices and rights

You can see and change your account information in the Service. To ask for a copy of your personal information, a correction or deletion, email privacy@pomeroyagency.com. If your information is in a business's books as Customer Data, we'll pass your request to that business, which decides on it. We won't treat you differently for making a request.

Cookies, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

This site sets one cookie, to keep you signed in, and places no advertising or analytics code. It does read, at sign-up only, the Google Analytics and Meta Pixel identifiers that pomeroyagency.com stored, as described under "Ad measurement". We honor Global Privacy Control as an opt-out of that sharing. Browsers' Do Not Track signal has no agreed meaning, so we rely on Global Privacy Control instead.

Children

The Service is for businesses and isn't directed to children under 16. We don't knowingly collect their information as account information.

Changes

When we change this policy, we post the new version here with a new date, and email account owners about material changes before they take effect.

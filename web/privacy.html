Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-27, in effect from October 27, 2026.

This policy explains how the company that operates Alt-QBO ("we" or "us") handles personal information in Alt-QBO (the "Service") and on this website. Alt-QBO is accounting and payroll software for businesses. Our Terms of Service also apply.

Two kinds of information

Account information is about the people who use the Service: name, email, password (stored only as a hash), two-factor settings, sign-in records, and the roles you hold. For the business that pays, it also includes billing contact and billing address. We decide how this information is used.

Customer Data is what a business puts into its books. It can include personal information about that business's employees, contractors and customers, such as names, addresses, Social Security numbers, pay and bank account details. We handle Customer Data only on the business's instructions, to provide the Service. The business decides what goes in and is responsible for its own notices to those people.

How we use it

  • To provide the Service: keeping books, calculating payroll and tax, preparing forms, and sending the reminders and receipts you'd expect.
  • To keep it secure: sign-in checks, two-factor, the audit log, encrypted nightly backups, and investigating misuse.
  • To bill for it, and to keep the records the law requires, such as invoices and tax records.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We don't show ads, and we don't use Customer Data to train outside AI models.

Who processes it for us

We use a small number of service providers, under contracts that limit them to providing their service to us:

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or to a successor if our business is sold, under the same protections as this policy.

Security

Social Security numbers, bank account numbers and two-factor secrets are encrypted at rest. Every viewing of a full number is logged. More is on our security page. If a breach affects your information, we notify you as California law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep account information while the account is open. After a subscription ends, its books stay readable for 7 years, free, so the records are there for a tax or payroll audit (Terms, section 10). A business can ask us to delete its data sooner; we then delete it within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep. Backups age out after 30 days.

Your choices and rights

You can see and change your account information in the Service. To ask for a copy of your personal information, a correction or deletion, email privacy@pomeroyagency.com. If your information is in a business's books as Customer Data, we'll pass your request to that business, which decides on it. We won't treat you differently for making a request.

Do Not Track

This site doesn't track you across other websites and uses no advertising or analytics cookies, so it responds to browser Do Not Track signals the same way it treats everyone: by not tracking. It uses one cookie, to keep you signed in.

Children

The Service is for businesses and isn't directed to children under 16. We don't knowingly collect their information as account information.

Changes

When we change this policy, we post the new version here with a new date, and email account owners about material changes before they take effect.

Referral Program Terms

Version 2026-11-03, in effect from November 3, 2026.

These Referral Program Terms ("Program Terms") are an agreement between Harmony in the Wild, LLC (doing business as Pomeroy Agency) ("we" or "us") and the accounting, bookkeeping, tax or HR firm that joins the TrailBook referral program ("Firm" or "you"). They are the partner firm addendum referred to in our Terms of Service, which also apply to your own use of TrailBook. The person joining confirms they have authority to bind the Firm.

1. How it works

1.1 When you join, you get a referral code and a sign-up link. A business becomes your "Referred Client" when it enters your code at sign-up or on its Billing page before its first payment to us. Each business can have only one referring firm. A business that has already paid us cannot become a Referred Client, unless we agree in writing.

1.2 You choose one reward, which applies to each Referred Client from the day it enters your code:

  • Client discount: the Referred Client pays 20% less on its TrailBook subscription fees for as long as it stays subscribed. Nothing is paid to you.
  • Referral fee: the Referred Client pays our standard price, and we pay you 20% of the subscription fees it actually pays us for billing periods that start within 12 months of its first paid period.

1.3 You can change your choice at any time. A change applies only to businesses that enter your code after it.

1.4 The Referred Client is always our customer, under our Terms of Service. It decides who may access its books, and it can remove you as its referring firm before its first payment.

2. The referral fee

2.1 The fee is figured on subscription fees only, after any discount and before sales and similar taxes. It does not include taxes, setup or other services, or amounts that are refunded, credited, charged back or never collected. If a payment that earned a fee is later refunded or charged back, we cancel the fee, or, if it was already paid, deduct it from later fees.

2.2 The fee is in the currency the Referred Client paid in (US or Canadian dollars).

2.3 We pay fees monthly, for fees earned in the previous month, by bank transfer, Interac e-Transfer or cheque. If the total owed to you is under 25 dollars in a currency, it carries over to the next month. You can see every fee and payment on the Referrals page in TrailBook.

2.4 Before we pay a US firm, you must give us a completed IRS Form W-9, and we may report fees paid on Form 1099-NEC. A firm outside the United States may need to give us a Form W-8BEN-E. You are responsible for your own income and other taxes on the fees. The fee includes any sales tax, GST/HST or similar tax that may apply to it; if you believe you must charge such a tax, tell us and invoice us before we pay.

2.5 No fee is earned for your own business, or a business owned or run by your owners, partners or employees, or for a referral that breaks these Program Terms.

3. Your obligations

3.1 Professional rules. You are responsible for following the laws and professional rules that apply to you, including any rule that requires you to tell a client about a referral fee or forbids you from accepting one. For example, CPA firms are subject to the AICPA Code of Professional Conduct (ET 1.520, Commissions and Referral Fees) and their state board's rules, which may forbid a fee from a client for whom the firm performs attest services. Choose the client discount where you cannot accept a fee. We show the reward that applies on each Referred Client's Billing page.

3.2 Honest marketing. Describe TrailBook accurately, use only claims we publish, and don't say you are part of, or speak for, us. Don't send unsolicited commercial email or messages about TrailBook, and follow anti-spam laws, including CAN-SPAM and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL). Don't bid on our names or trademarks in paid search ads.

3.3 Independence. You are an independent business. Nothing in these Program Terms makes you our agent, employee or partner. You may not make promises or agreements for us.

4. What you can see

For each Referred Client you can see its business name, whether it is in a trial, paying or ended, and the fees it has earned you. You cannot see its books unless the client invites you to its organisation. Use this information only to manage the referral relationship.

5. Changes and ending

5.1 We may change or end the program, or these Program Terms, with 30 days' notice by email. Fees earned before the change or end stay payable under the terms that applied when they were earned.

5.2 Either of us may leave the program at any time. If we end your participation because you broke these Program Terms, fees not yet paid for the referrals involved are forfeited.

5.3 Sections 2.1, 2.4, 3 and 5 to 6 survive the end of your participation.

6. General

6.1 Our total liability under these Program Terms is limited to the fees owed to you under them. Sections 14 (Limitation of liability) and 16 (Governing law and disputes) of our Terms of Service apply to these Program Terms.

6.2 Notices to us go to support@pomeroyagency.com. Notices to you go to the payout email in your referral settings, or the account owner's email.

Our promises

Accounting software should be boring in the best way: fair prices, books that can't be quietly changed, and your records always within reach. These promises are part of our terms of service, so they bind us.

Price and billing

  • No user limits and no invoice limits, ever, on any plan.
  • Prices change at most once a year, by no more than 10%, with 60 days' notice by email.
  • The first 100 businesses to subscribe keep their price for 24 months.
  • Cancel any time from the Billing page, no phone call. A yearly plan cancelled within 60 days of paying is refunded for the unused months.

Your data

  • Your books are yours. Download a full backup any time, on any plan, including after you cancel.
  • After you cancel, your books stay readable for 7 years, free, so your records are there for an IRS, EDD or CDTFA audit.
  • We never sell your data, show you ads, or use your books to train outside AI models.

Books that can't be quietly changed

  • Every entry balances, and posted entries are never edited: corrections are reversals you can see. Closed months stay closed. The database enforces this, not just the screens.
  • An audit trail records who changed what and when. Each event is chained to the last with a SHA-256 hash, so an edit or deletion shows.

Staying compliant

  • Federal and California tax and labor rules are checked every day against the IRS, SSA, Department of Labor, EDD, DIR, CDTFA and the California Legislature. A person reviews each change before it goes live.
  • We say plainly what we don't do: we don't file your returns or move your payroll money. TrailBook prepares; you or your accountant file and pay.

Questions

What happens after the trial? Add a card and keep going on the plan you picked. If you don't, nothing is deleted: your books stay readable and downloadable, and only changes pause until you subscribe.

Can I cancel? Any time, from the Billing page, with no phone call. You keep full use until the end of the period you paid for. A yearly plan cancelled within 60 days of paying is refunded for the unused months.

Will the price go up? At most once a year, by no more than 10%, with 60 days' notice by email. The first 100 businesses to subscribe keep their price for 24 months.

Do I pay sales tax? If your business is in California: from January 1, 2027, California taxes software subscriptions, so your invoice adds sales tax at your address's rate. Before then there is none. In other states, we add sales tax only where we are required to collect it. Extras like setup calls are services and are billed separately.

I'm an accountant or HR firm. Your firm's own logins are free, and one sign-in switches between all your clients' books. Clients you bring get 20% off for as long as they stay, or your firm can take a 20% referral fee for their first 12 months instead. The client is always the customer of record.

Who can use TrailBook? Businesses based in one of the 47 US states TrailBook supports so far: California, Texas, Florida, Nevada, Tennessee, South Dakota, Wyoming, New Hampshire, Alaska, Washington, Arizona, Oregon, New York, Illinois, Colorado, North Carolina, Georgia, Utah, Idaho, Michigan, Virginia, Massachusetts, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Ohio, Indiana, Maryland, Missouri, Wisconsin, Minnesota, South Carolina, Alabama, Louisiana, Kentucky, Oklahoma, Connecticut, Iowa, Arkansas, Delaware, Kansas, Mississippi, North Dakota, Montana, New Mexico, Rhode Island, Vermont and West Virginia. Each comes with its state payroll rules built in, plus local taxes where they apply: New York City and Yonkers income tax, Pennsylvania earned income tax, Local Services Tax and the Philadelphia wage tax, Ohio city and school district taxes, Indiana and Maryland county taxes, the Kansas City and St. Louis earnings tax, Alabama and Kentucky local occupational taxes (entered for each employee), the Wilmington, Delaware earned income tax, Minnesota, Connecticut and Delaware paid leave contributions, Rhode Island temporary disability insurance, the Vermont Child Care Contribution, and city minimum wages. Michigan city income taxes, such as Detroit's, aren't withheld yet. More states are being added, and Canada is planned. Sales tax rates are looked up automatically in California; in the other states you set your rate yourself.
